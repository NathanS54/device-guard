Getting started

    Guard

    Starting…

    Off: the guard stays silent, which is right for libraries and quiet places. On: a siren plays if someone unknown keeps moving the laptop after an alert, until the camera sees your face. Check your volume with Test sound, and disconnect headphones before you leave, or the siren plays in them.

                  How the camera looks for faces. Most people never need to change this.

                  Standard finds faces a table's length away and more. Fast only sees faces close to the laptop; try it if the frames-per-second counter in the camera's corner stays low on an older computer.

                  About

                  Your data

                  Face recognition runs in this browser, and your face data and recordings are stored only on this laptop. The one exception: if you pair a phone, alert clips are uploaded locked (only your paired phones have the key, not even the site's owner) and deleted after 7 days.

                  Welcome to Device Guard

                  Device Guard watches your laptop through its camera while you're away.

                  1. Enroll your face so it knows you. Add trusted people, like a roommate, if you want.
                  2. Arm the guard before you step away.
                  3. If someone it doesn't know moves your laptop, it quietly saves a recording. It turns off when it sees you again.

                  What it needs

                  • Chrome or Edge on a laptop with a camera.
                  • This page open and the lid up. It can't watch while the laptop is closed or asleep.

                  Use it fairly

                  • While this page is open, it keeps the last few minutes of video (no sound) and deletes older minutes. Don't point it at places where people expect privacy.
                  • Ask friends before you add their faces.
                  • Your face and recordings stay on this laptop. If you pair a phone, only alert clips go to it, locked with a key only your phone has, and they're deleted after 7 days. Delete everything anytime in Settings.
